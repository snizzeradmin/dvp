Why Aegis Vote?

Built for Trust, Designed for People

Every feature is designed with integrity and accessibility in mind

⛓

Blockchain Immutability

Votes are written to the Ethereum blockchain and cannot be changed, deleted, or manipulated by anyone — including administrators.

🦊

MetaMask Identity

Your Ethereum wallet is your digital identity. Only the wallet linked to your registered account can cast your vote.

🔍

Transparent Verification

Every voter receives a transaction hash after voting. Anyone can verify their vote exists on the public blockchain at any time.

🛡

Duplicate Prevention

Smart contract logic enforces one vote per wallet at the protocol level. No code change or database manipulation can bypass this.

📊

Real-Time Results

Results are read directly from the smart contract — live, accurate, and impossible to falsify. No manual vote counting needed.

🔐

Zero-Trust Architecture

Every request is validated. Passwords are bcrypt-hashed. JWT tokens secure sessions. All critical decisions happen on-chain.

How Voting Works

Simple for voters, secure by design

📝
Register

Create your account with your name, email, government ID, and Ethereum wallet address.

✅
Get Approved

An election administrator verifies your identity and authorizes your wallet address on the blockchain.

🦊
Connect Wallet

Open the voting page, click "Connect Wallet", and approve the MetaMask connection request.

🗳
Cast Your Vote

Select your candidate, confirm your choice, and approve the blockchain transaction in MetaMask.

⛓
Verified on Chain

Your vote is permanently recorded. You receive a transaction hash as proof that you can verify anytime.

Security

Zero Compromises on Security

Aegis Vote was built with the assumption that every client request could be malicious. Every layer of the system is independently secure.

✓
Blockchain as Source of Truth

The backend never trusts itself to decide if someone has voted. All critical checks happen on the smart contract.

✓
bcrypt Password Hashing

Passwords are hashed with bcrypt at cost factor 12 and are never stored or transmitted in plain text.

✓
Wallet Ownership Verification

The connected MetaMask wallet is verified against your registered address before any vote is submitted.

✓
JWT Authentication

All protected endpoints require a valid JWT token. Expired and invalid tokens are immediately rejected.
