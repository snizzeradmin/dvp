Daily care checklist
Watch for danger signs
⚠ Seek care immediately if you see
  • Yellowing of skin or eyes (jaundice) that's spreading or deepening
  • Fever, or the baby feels unusually cold
  • Difficulty breathing or fast, grunting breaths
  • Refusing to feed, or no wet nappies for 6+ hours
  • Unusual limpness, or a bulging soft spot
  • Redness, swelling, or discharge from the cord stump
